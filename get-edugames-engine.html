Learner guide & controls

How to play.

Reach the exit of each level. Knowledge gates block the path until you answer the science question in front of them.

Controls

A ←Move left
D →Move right
Space W ↑Jump (hold for height)
P EscPause
MSound on / off
1–4Select a question answer

On phones and tablets, use the on-screen movement and jump buttons. Question dialogs are touch-friendly.

The loop

  1. 1
    Explore

    Move through the level and collect glowing sample vials for bonus points.

  2. 2
    Answer

    Touch a terminal to open a knowledge gate. A correct answer awards points and opens the barrier; a wrong answer costs one life and opens the gate anyway, with the explanation shown.

  3. 3
    Survive

    Avoid spills and unstable zones. Falling into a gap costs a life and returns you to the last checkpoint.

  4. 4
    Finish

    Reach the sequencer at the end of the level. Unlock achievements and compare your best score — stored only on this device.

Scoring

  • Correct answer100–220 pts
  • Streak bonus+20 per answer
  • Sample vial+25 pts
  • Level completion200 + time + lives

Difficult questions are worth more. Score does not measure scientific competence — it is a practice motivator.

Accessibility & privacy

  • Keyboard-only play is supported.
  • Sound is optional; it can be muted at any time.
  • The game respects prefers-reduced-motion for decorative animation.
  • No account, name, email or learner data is requested.
  • Best scores and unlocked achievements are saved in localStorage only. Clear site data to erase them.

Scientific integrity

Science note.

GET EduGames is an educational project of Geneticist Expertise Team (GET), a Jordanian scientific and voluntary initiative established in 2014.

What this is

An introductory, game-based practice tool for genetics, mutations, laboratory biosafety and medical laboratory science concepts. Each question is followed by a short explanation so that a wrong answer becomes a learning moment.

Content is deliberately introductory. It is not a curriculum, a certification pathway, or a substitute for supervised laboratory training.

What this is not

Not a diagnostic or medical decision tool. Nothing here should be used to diagnose, treat, counsel or make clinical decisions about any person. No patient data is requested or stored.

Questions and explanations marked with the amber review badge are drafts pending confirmation by a qualified subject-matter expert.

Content status

Draft for expert review

The starter content was written for this prototype and requires GET scientific review before public or classroom use.

Explanations included

Every question carries a rationale, not only a correct answer key.
